Who I am

Hi, I'm Margarita.

Margarita Naydenova  ·  Founder

The beginning

I started out in Biochemistry — fascinated by living systems and the cellular rules of life. Over five years I built a grounding in Biotechnology, Genetics and Microbiology, and co-authored a peer-reviewed paper. But I knew academia wasn't where I was meant to stay.

The turn

Right after my MSc I moved into Consulting — and discovered an unexpected passion: data, visualisation, and presentation design.Four years in, I became a Senior Consultant at Analysys Mason, working with FMCG and Fortune 500 clients across Europe.

What I do now

Today I run De Novo Consulting independently — the most energising chapter yet. I help companies find clarity in complex data, build dashboards and reports people actually use, and design presentations that make an impact.
